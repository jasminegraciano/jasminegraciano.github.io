Our story

From small beginnings to a trusted Connecticut painting company.

Javier Santos, owner of Connecticut House Painters LLC, has been serving Connecticut residents with quality painting service since 2010. The business started small, without a company name, but with a clear focus on doing painting work the right way.

In 2010, the team began operating as Masters Touch Painting and Remodeling LLC. Their work took them around New England and eventually to Orlando, Florida, where the company continued to grow under that name. When it came time to name the Connecticut company, Javier chose Connecticut House Painters because it honored the way the business first began.

Today, Connecticut House Painters LLC is not limited to house painting only. The company also specializes in commercial services, power washing, refinishing, deck staining, repainting, and related painting work for properties of many sizes.

We do more than brush or roll paint on walls. We focus on high-quality painting, careful prep, clean job sites, and affordable service customers can feel confident about.

Color and protection designed for outdoor wood.

Deck stain penetrates or coats prepared wood to improve its appearance and reduce exposure to moisture and ultraviolet light. Unlike a quick cosmetic coat, a lasting result depends on the wood species, previous finish, weathering, cleaning, drying time, and the right product.

Our team evaluates those conditions before recommending a transparent, semi-transparent, or more solid finish.

Our deck staining process

The finish is only as good as the preparation beneath it.

Every project is adjusted for the species, age, previous coating, exposure, and current condition of the wood.

1

Evaluate the wood and coating

We review wear, previous stain, moisture, organic growth, damaged areas, and the desired finished appearance.

2

Clean and prepare carefully

The deck is cleaned using an approach appropriate for the wood, followed by additional preparation where required.

3

Allow proper drying conditions

Moisture and weather matter. Stain is scheduled only when the surface and forecast support reliable application.

4

Apply the selected finish

The stain is applied for controlled coverage, clean edges, and the appearance chosen for the project.

Choosing a finish

Let the grain show or add more color and coverage.

Transparent

Highlights natural grain with minimal added color where suitable for the wood and condition.

Semi-transparent

Adds tone while allowing much of the wood character to remain visible.

Solid color

Offers a more uniform painted appearance and higher color coverage for appropriate surfaces.

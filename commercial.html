How we protect the project

From estimate to final walk-through.

Connecticut House Painters LLC brings professional prep, clean-up, safety standards, and follow-up to every commercial painting project.

Free commercial painting estimates

Clear project planning from the start.

Following your request for a free estimate, a commercial painting professional visits your business or property to discuss your project in detail, help with color selection and set a date for prep work. Written estimates are provided.

Commercial prep and clean up services

Prepared surfaces and organized sites.

Industrial and commercial locations are assessed and prepared by a team of experts. Surface preparation includes hot/cold power washing, sandblasting and any other necessary treatments. Surrounding landscapes and commercial property are safeguarded and project sites are left clean and organized.

Taking care of businesses

Certified crews and commercial products.

Connecticut House Painters LLC painting crews are certified and qualified to provide service for a variety of commercial projects that include high rise exterior painting and hard-to-access interior areas. Crews use advanced equipment operated under the strictest of safety standards. Industrial grade paints and products are carefully adjusted for each project for cost effectiveness and maximum durability.

Project completion and follow-up

Final touch-ups before the job is complete.

Our job is not complete until you're satisfied. Our residential painting team in Waterford will inspect each work area for final touch-ups until the final walk-through and inspection.

Our cabinet process

Beautiful results begin long before the final coat.

Cabinets are touched every day, so preparation matters. We build the finish around adhesion, smoothness, clean lines, and long-term use.

1

Protect and organize

Work areas, floors, counters, appliances, and nearby surfaces are carefully protected before preparation begins.

2

Clean, sand, and repair

Grease and residue are removed, surfaces are prepared for adhesion, and minor imperfections are addressed.

4

Apply and inspect the finish

Durable finish coats are applied, details are reviewed, and doors and drawers are carefully reassembled.
